# Chore Buddies

A cute chore and learning world for kids (kindergarten to 8th grade): chores earn coins, and kids explore a little world full of places, games, a house to decorate, a salon, outfits and more. It works on iPhone, iPad and computers.

- **Play it:** open `index.html` from your own website. See **SETUP.md** for the free website (Cloudflare Pages) and saved family accounts (Supabase).
- **Files:** `app.html` is the whole app. `build.sh` turns it into `index.html`. `voice/` holds the recorded voice clips. `supabase/` holds the cloud account setup.
